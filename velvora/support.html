Velvora

Velvora puts retro film styles on your photos: twenty-two looks built from classic film stocks, eight cameras, and a full set of editing controls, on Mac, iPhone and iPad. It is sold on the App Store as Velvora Retro Photo. Everything runs on your device, so it works without a connection.

Opening a photo

On iPhone and iPad, tap Choose a Photo to pick from your library, or Open a File to open an image from Files. The picker needs no permission; the app asks for library access when you open a photo for editing, so that it can save the edit back onto it.

On a Mac, use Open Image…, drop a file on the app, or choose Open With → Velvora in the Finder. From Photos, select a photo and choose Image → Edit With → Velvora.

Saving

Your original is never overwritten. Save puts the edit onto the photo as a Photos edit, so you can revert it in Photos at any time, and reopening the photo in Velvora brings your sliders back. Save as Copy makes a duplicate in the Velvora album and leaves the original alone. Export on the Mac and Share on iPhone and iPad write a finished JPEG that carries the photo’s own metadata.

iCloud Photos carries an edit between your devices, and a photo graded on one opens with the same sliders on the other. On a device without Velvora installed, Photos shows the finished picture; use Revert to Original there to edit it again.

Looks and cameras

Each look was built from the published behavior of a real film stock and is named for what it does rather than for the stock. A camera adds a lens and a frame: picking one crops the photo to that camera’s shape, and you can drag the picture around inside the frame. The Lens slider scales the camera’s character from the real thing down to a perfect lens, and Strength blends the whole look back toward the original.

Good to know

Velvora edits still photos; a Live Photo opens as its still. HDR photos are rendered as standard-range JPEGs. RAW files are opened with default settings. One photo is edited at a time.

Reporting an issue

If something isn’t working, a look renders strangely, or you have a feature request, please email hello@spice-lick.com. Including your device and its OS version, the look and camera you had on, the kind of file (HEIC, JPEG, RAW, TIFF), and whether the photo came from Photos or from a file helps us track it down faster. A screenshot or an export that shows the problem is even better.

Purchases and refunds

Velvora is a one-time purchase billed through your Apple ID, and one purchase covers the Mac, iPhone and iPad apps. There are no subscriptions or in-app purchases. For refunds, use Apple’s Report a Problem page. Refunds are issued at Apple’s discretion under the App Store terms.
