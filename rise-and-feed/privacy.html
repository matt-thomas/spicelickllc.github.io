Privacy Policy

Rise & Feed

Last updated: October 27, 2026

Rise & Feed is published by Spice Lick LLC. We designed this app to require no account and to collect no personal information. This page explains what that means in practice.

Information we do not collect

We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal information. There are no user accounts, no analytics, no advertising identifiers, and no third-party tracking SDKs in the app.

Location

If you grant location permission, the app uses your device’s location to calculate sun and moon times and solunar periods for where you are. All of those calculations run on your device. Your location is never transmitted to us, and it is not stored beyond the place you have chosen in the app.

Location permission is optional. You can instead search for a place or drop a pin on a map, and the app works the same way.

Network requests

For places in the United States, the app fetches a weather forecast and any active weather alerts from the National Weather Service (api.weather.gov). To do that it sends the coordinates of the place you have chosen, rounded to about one kilometer. The National Weather Service uses them only to answer the request. We have no access to those requests. The request identifies the app and this website so the National Weather Service can reach us if needed; it contains nothing about you.

Place search, map display, and place names use Apple’s MapKit and Core Location services. Those requests are governed by Apple’s privacy policy.

Standard network metadata (such as your IP address) may be visible to the National Weather Service or Apple as part of normal HTTPS traffic, but we have no access to it.

Local storage

The app keeps your settings, your chosen place, and a short list of recently used places on your device so it can work offline. It also caches the most recent forecast for a short time. This data stays on your device and is removed when you delete the app.

Purchases

Rise & Feed is a paid app with no in-app purchases or subscriptions. Your purchase is processed by Apple through the App Store; we never see your payment method, billing address, or Apple ID.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If we change how the app handles data, we will update this page and revise the date at the top.
