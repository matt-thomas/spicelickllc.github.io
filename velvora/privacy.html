Privacy Policy

Velvora

Last updated: November 6, 2026

Velvora is published by Spice Lick LLC for Mac, iPhone and iPad. We designed this app to require no account and to collect no personal information. This page explains what that means in practice.

Information we do not collect

We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal information. There are no user accounts, no analytics, no advertising identifiers, and no third-party tracking SDKs in the app.

Velvora makes no network requests at all. Every look is rendered on your device, and nothing you do in the app leaves it.

Your photos

Velvora works on the photos you choose. On iPhone and iPad it asks for access to your photo library only when you open a photo for editing, because that is what lets it save the edit back onto that photo and bring your settings back the next time you open it. Browsing the picker needs no permission. On a Mac, Photos hands the app a copy of the photo you chose to edit, or you open an image file yourself.

Your originals are never overwritten. An edit saved to Photos is a Photos edit, which you can revert there at any time, and a copy goes into the Velvora album as a new photo. The app never uploads, copies, or shares a photo except where you ask it to: Save, Save as Copy, Export, or Share.

Photo metadata

A photo’s own metadata, such as the capture date, the camera and lens, and any location the camera recorded, stays with the photo and is carried into the copies and exports you make, as it is in Photos. On the Mac, Velvora shows some of it in its About section. It is not used for anything else and is not sent anywhere. If you would rather an export not carry a location, remove it in Photos first.

Local storage

The app remembers a few preferences on your device, such as which control the Adjust tab last showed and, on the Mac, where its windows were. Your edits themselves are kept by Photos as part of each photo. The preferences are removed when you delete the app.

Purchases

Velvora has no in-app purchases or subscriptions. Any purchase is processed by Apple through the App Store; we never see your payment method, billing address, or Apple ID.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If we change how the app handles data, we will update this page and revise the date at the top.
