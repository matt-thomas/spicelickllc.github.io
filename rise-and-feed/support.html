Rise & Feed

Rise & Feed is an iOS app that shows solunar periods, sun and moon times, and moon phase for your location or any place you choose, in day, week, and month views. All of the astronomy runs on your device, so it works offline and for any date.

How the ratings work

Major periods are two hours centered on the moon overhead and underfoot. Minor periods are one hour centered on moonrise and moonset. Each day gets a rating from the moon phase and from how close its periods fall to sunrise and sunset. Where a forecast is available, separate fishing and hunting ratings add classic weather signals such as falling pressure, high wind, and temperature swings.

These ratings are rules of thumb drawn from angling and hunting lore, not a prediction or a guarantee. Local conditions, season, and regulations always come first.

Weather

Forecasts come from the National Weather Service and cover only the United States and its territories. For other places the app shows solunar and astronomical data without weather. The forecast needs an internet connection and reaches about a week ahead; pressure data runs two to three days. We are not affiliated with the National Weather Service. For warnings and safety decisions, check weather.gov or local authorities directly.

Location

Location permission is optional. If you prefer not to grant it, search for a place or drop a pin on the map. Times are shown in the chosen place’s own time zone.

Reporting an issue

If something isn’t working, a time looks wrong, or you have a feature request, please email hello@spice-lick.com. Including your iOS version, the place and date you were looking at, and a brief description of the issue helps us track it down faster.
